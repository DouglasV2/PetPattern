## Full description

**Remember what changed. Not a diagnosis — a clearer history.**

PetPattern is a low-effort health timeline for pets with recurring, unclear skin,
digestive, or routine-related issues. Set what's normal once, then log only what
changes — most days take a few seconds. Every entry builds a clear, dated record
you can hand your vet.

**Useful from day one**
- Set your pet's baseline once; a quick daily check-in is enough.
- "No change since last check-in" or "Back to usual" — one tap, honest either way.
- A vet-ready summary exists from your very first log.

**A record, not a black box**
- A readable timeline of what changed, when, and what stayed steady.
- A weekly recap of the facts — useful even when no pattern appears.
- When something looks like it might line up, PetPattern shows the evidence AND
  what limits it — what repeated, what didn't, and what the record can't tell you.

**Honest about uncertainty**
- PetPattern does not diagnose and is not a substitute for veterinary care.
- It never claims a cause or an allergy. It shows what was observed, what
  repeated across separate periods, and what remains uncertain — so you and your
  vet can look at the same clear record.

**Private and calm**
- No ads, no third-party tracking. No streak guilt or dark patterns.
- Your notes stay yours.

_PetPattern is a record-keeping tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a
veterinarian. If your pet is unwell, contact your vet._
