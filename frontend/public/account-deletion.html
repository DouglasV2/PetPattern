Delete your PetPattern account

You can delete your account and your pets' records at any time — either inside the app, or by asking us, if you no longer have the app installed.

In the app

  1. Open PetPattern and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Under Delete account, choose Delete my account.
  4. Confirm both prompts.

This is immediate and cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you have uninstalled PetPattern or cannot sign in, email privacy@petpattern.app from the email address on the account, with the subject Account deletion request.

We reply to confirm it is really you before deleting anything. We do not ask for your password — no one at PetPattern will ever request it.

Requests are actioned within 30 days, and usually within a few days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • Every pet record you own: daily check-ins, food and medication records, notes and photos.
  • Any vet-share links you created (they stop working immediately).

What is not deleted

A small amount of pseudonymous, aggregate usage data — for example that a check-in screen was opened on a given day, and a pet's species — is kept to understand how PetPattern is used overall. It never contains your name, email, notes, or your pet's health details, and because it is anonymous it cannot be linked back to you or your pet.

If someone else was invited to help care for a pet, deleting the pet or your account ends their access to it.

Export first, if you want a copy

Deletion is permanent. If you would like to keep your pet's history, use Account → Export my data before deleting; it downloads everything PetPattern stores for you as a JSON file.

PetPattern is a private health notebook for pets. It does not diagnose, and it does not replace veterinary care.
